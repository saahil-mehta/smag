## Permanent Magnetic Lifter (priority: lifting family)

- Suggested slug: `permanent-magnetic-lifter`
- Names used in sources: Permanent Magnet Lifter, Permanent Lifting Magnet, S-MAG neoLIFT, Maxx Series

A hand-operated lifting magnet for moving finished or rough ferrous loads, flat or round, with a crane or hoist. The magnet is switched on and off by a locking hand lever, so one person can attach and release loads without slings or clamps. Powered by neodymium iron boron magnets, it needs no electricity and no maintenance.

Specs (from the printed neoLIFT/Maxx brochure, the most authoritative source):
- 12 models, Maxx-100 to Maxx-6000, rated 100 kg to 6000 kg (flat load)
- Round load rating is 50% of the flat rating
- Every magnet tested to lift 3 times the safe working load (safety factor 3)
- Two-pole design works on both flat and round material
- Safety locking mechanism on the handle prevents accidental release
- Maximum operating temperature 80 deg C (IndiaMART listing)
- 3 year guarantee (brochure claim, conditions apply)

## Magnetic Floor Sweeper (priority)

- Suggested slug: `magnetic-floor-sweeper`
- Names used in sources: Floor Magnetic Sweeper, Magnetic Floor Sweeper, Magnetic Floor Sweepers (push type)

A push-along sweeper with a permanent magnet bar between two wheels, used to clear nails, screws, swarf and other ferrous debris from floors and yards. Collected metal is released with a quick-release lever, so the head never needs to be picked clean by hand. No power source is required.

Specs:
- Sweeping widths listed: 12 inch, 24 inch and 72 inch versions (the 72 inch figure appears on one listing and may describe a wide or towed variant; confirm with SMAG)
- Push type, manual operation with quick-release lever
- Handle length 4-5 ft
- Frame: steel or aluminium
- Rubber wheels, 8-12 inch diameter
- Brand S-MAG, made in India

Applications: factory floors, workshops, warehouses, driveways and car parks, construction sites, tyre-puncture prevention around loading areas.

Pricing found (FLAG, do not publish): Rs 7,500/piece.

Images:
- `magnetic-floor-sweeper-01.png`: sweeper in use on a concrete floor scattered with pins and swarf. Best hero.
- `magnetic-floor-sweeper-02.png`: sweeper studio shot, white body with black handle.

## Table Top Demagnetiser (priority)

- Suggested slug: `table-top-demagnetiser`
- Names used in sources: Demagnetization Unit (Maxx-Demag), Demagnetizer 230 Volts Ac

A bench-mounted plate demagnetiser for removing residual magnetism from tools, components and gauges after machining on magnetic chucks. The part is passed across the top plate and comes away demagnetised. Runs on single-phase mains.

Specs (Maxx-Demag bench unit):
- Model: Maxx-Demag
- Plate size 150 x 100 mm
- 230 V AC, single phase, 50 Hz
- A second, larger listing (Demagnetizer 230 Volts AC) quotes 500 x 500 mm, 415 V AC, 30-42 A, continuous 100% duty; treat this as the heavy industrial surface demagnetiser variant and confirm figures with SMAG, as the two listings conflict

Applications: tool rooms and grinding shops (after magnetic chuck use), bearing and gauge servicing, any process where residual magnetism attracts swarf.

Pricing found (FLAG, do not publish): Rs 2,500/piece.

Images:
- `table-top-demagnetiser-01.png`: SMAG Maxx-Demag bench demagnetiser, orange housing with indicator lamp and top plate. Only photo found; usable as hero (slightly soft focus).

## Notes and gaps

- All IndiaMART product copy is AI-written; everything above is reworded and should be reworded again if it still reads generic. Do not lift phrasing from the PDFs.
- Conflicting data to confirm with SMAG: sweeper widths (12/24/72 inch across three listings), demagnetiser size and voltage (150x100 mm 230 V vs 500x500 mm 415 V), round chuck "Max RPM / Power Source" fields (listed nonsensically as "UPTO 100 R CM"), and lifter listing (100 kg load / neodymium / 80 deg C) vs brochure table (authoritative).
- The "Magnetic Pick Up Tool Manufacturers in India" PDF is a competitor search-results page. Only the SMAG Mini Magnetic Collector entry was used; all other photos and specs on it belong to other companies and were discarded.
- The Photos PDF contains several images that appear to be generic catalogue/stock composites (blue-background magnet assortments, some chuck and lifter shots). The SMAG-branded photos were preferred wherever possible; treat unbranded studio shots with mild caution regarding provenance.
- No photo gaps for the priority products in my set: lifter, floor sweeper, alnico power magnets, NdFeB, pot magnets, rectangular and round chucks, and table top demagnetiser all have at least one usable image. Weakest coverage: the demagnetiser (one slightly soft photo) and the mini magnetic collector (one photo).
- Products in my PDFs without their own usable photo: Sintered Ferrite Magnet as a distinct item (covered by the ferrite assortment shots), "Magnetic Sticks/Pens" style pick-up tools (SMAG does not list one), and the Electro Permanent / Multicoil Electromagnetic Chuck (no distinct photo found; the orange unit in the NdFeB PDF is the demagnetiser, not the chuck).
