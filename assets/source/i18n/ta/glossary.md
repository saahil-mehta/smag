# Tamil (ta) glossary

Register: the spoken business Tamil of Tamil Nadu factories. English trade
words are written in Tamil script the way engineers say them. Address the
reader as நீங்கள். Names, model and series names (Maxx, Premier, Maxx-Clean,
Filtramag, neoLIFT), grades, codes and units stay in Latin letters.
