# Marathi (mr) glossary

Devanagari the way they are said on the shop floor. Names, models, grades,
codes and units stay in Latin letters as STYLE.md says.

## Units

Units and numbers stay as written in Latin (mm, kg, gauss, bar, inch, ft, deg C,
N35, 304). Spelled-out "degrees Celsius" is written डिग्री सेल्सियस. "feet" is
kept as `feet`.
