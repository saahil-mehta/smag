## Overview
### A filter you clean and put back
Fluid enters the housing, passes over a cluster of high strength magnetic rods and leaves clean. Iron and steel particles hold to the rods, including the fine wear particles that pass through a paper or bag filter.

The housing does not block as it loads, so the flow to the machine stays constant. When the rods are full, lift the assembly out, wipe it clean and refit it. Nothing is thrown away.

Used on grinding machines, machining centres, honing and lapping machines, wire cut machines and parts washers. Suitable for water based coolants, neat oils, hydraulic oil and wash solutions. Stainless steel construction suits corrosive fluids.
