## Overview
### Built for the transmission line
Ultrafiltrex is a modular magnetic filter for oil, gas, petrochemical and fuel pipelines. Ferrous contamination holds to the neodymium cores as the flow passes. Barrier filters downstream stop blocking, and abrasive wear on compressors, pumps, valves and meters falls.

Units are assembled in modules to suit the flow rate. Quick release fittings let the cores be lifted out and cleaned. The stainless steel body is built for a long service life.

We can sample the pipeline and analyse the contamination, size the unit for your flow and connections, and advise on installation, lifting gear for cleaning, and operator training.
