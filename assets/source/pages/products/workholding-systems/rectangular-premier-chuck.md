## Overview
### Fast, even, safe clamping
Place the part on the top plate and turn the lever. The hold is instant and the same across the whole plate. The part is held on one face, so the wheel can reach the other five.

A partial position on the lever gives a light hold for setting the part before the full hold is applied. The top plate is thick enough to be reground many times, so the chuck keeps its accuracy for years.

Made to the size of your machine table. Electromagnetic and multicoil electromagnetic chucks are built to order for very large plates.
