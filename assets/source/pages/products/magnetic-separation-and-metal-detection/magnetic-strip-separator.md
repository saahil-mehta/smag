## Tagline
A hand-held magnet for batch checks and clean-up

## Bullets
- Strontium ferrite block in a 304 stainless cover
- Lift the cover and the collected iron drops off
- Rectangular and round faces, two sizes
- Light enough for one hand
- About 1,700 gauss at the face

## Overview
### Sweep, lift, release
Sweep the face over the material and iron collects on the cover. Lift the cover away from the magnet and the iron drops into a bin. Hands never touch sharp swarf.

Used for batch checks on raw material in foundries, plastics, scrap and chemicals, and for clearing benches and floors around the works.
