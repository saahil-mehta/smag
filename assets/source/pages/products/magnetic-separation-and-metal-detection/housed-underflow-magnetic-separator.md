## Overview
### Separation inside the duct
When the product runs inside closed ducting, the separator has to live in the line. This housing bolts between flanges and puts staggered rows of rods in the path of everything that passes.

Cleaning works like our bullet housing. Unclamp the door, draw the rods out, wipe them and slide them back. The duct joints are never broken. Rods are available from 7,000 to 13,500 gauss.
