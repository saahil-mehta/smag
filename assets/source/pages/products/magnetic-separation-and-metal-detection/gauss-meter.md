## Overview
### Proof that the magnets still work
Magnet strength falls if a separator is overheated or knocked hard. An audit needs a number, and the number should be compared with the tested certificate supplied with the unit.

Press the probe to the rod or plate face and read the meter. Log the figure against the certificate. We use the same meters to certify every separator that leaves the works, and supply them to customers who run their own yearly checks.
