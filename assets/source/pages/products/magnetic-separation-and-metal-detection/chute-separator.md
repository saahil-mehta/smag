## Tagline
A hinged plate magnet for angled chutes

## Bullets
- Mounts on the inside face of a vertical or angled chute
- Deep field pulls iron out of the sliding product
- Hinged door swings open for cleaning
- LM-6 cast aluminium or SS 304/316 encapsulation
- Made to the chute size

## Overview
### The chute does the work
The plate bolts to the chute face and acts on the product sliding past. There is nothing to drive and no running cost. Cleaning is a wipe of the plate face with the door swung open.

Used ahead of mills, sieves and packing lines in textiles, tea, coffee, chemicals and plastics. Made to your chute dimensions.
