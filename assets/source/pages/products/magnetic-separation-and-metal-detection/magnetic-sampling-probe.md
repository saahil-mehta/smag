## Overview
### A quick answer on contamination
Push the probe into the material and draw it out. Any iron in the batch is now on the sleeve. Slide the magnet core out of the sleeve and the iron drops onto a tray for inspection or weighing.

Used as a routine check on incoming material, and as a fast way to find which stage of a line is shedding metal. A clean probe after the final separator is useful evidence for an auditor.
