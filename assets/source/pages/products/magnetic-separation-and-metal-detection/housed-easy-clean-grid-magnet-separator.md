A sealed housing with a magnetic grid that lifts out for cleaning

## Bullets
- Fits into an existing chute or hopper outlet
- For dry powders and granules on gravity lines
- Rods from 7,000 to 13,500 gauss
- Grid lifts out of the housing in one piece
- Single, double, triple and quadruple row builds

## Overview
### Fine iron removed inside a closed housing
The product falls through a hood, passes over the grid and leaves through a cone. The housing is sealed between the hood and the cone, so no dust escapes and nothing gets in. The rods hold nails, wire, rust and fine wear particles.

To clean, release the clamps and lift the grid out. The collected iron is wiped off and the grid goes back in. A double or triple row housing gives more rods in the product path for higher throughput or finer particles.

Common positions are under a silo, at a hopper outlet, and just before packing. The housing is made to your line size in 304 stainless steel and comes with a tested certificate of magnetic strength.
