## Overview
### Simple protection on a gravity line
Product falls through the grid. Every path between the rods passes within a few millimetres of a magnet face, so nails, wire, swarf and fine iron hold to the rods while the product carries on.

A one stage grid has a single row of rods. A two stage grid has two rows, offset, so nothing can fall straight through. Two stage grids are used where the particles are fine or the flow is fast.

Grids are lifted out for cleaning and wiped by hand. They are used at intake hoppers, above mixers and mills, and at bagging points in food, plastics, chemicals and minerals.
