## Tagline
Rod traps that pull fine iron out of a liquid line

## Bullets
- Fits into a pipeline with flanged connections
- Neodymium rods in watertight 304 stainless sheaths
- In-line trap with 7 rods, housed trap with 9 rods
- Single wall or jacketed for heated lines
- Cleaned by lifting the rod cluster out

## Overview
### Protection for pumps, valves and the product
Liquid enters the trap, passes over a cluster of magnetic rods and leaves through the outlet. Iron and fine wear particles hold to the rods. Nothing is consumed and the flow is unrestricted.

The in-line trap has a 75 mm rod cluster, 250 mm long, with 7 rods. The housed trap has a 150 mm cluster with 9 rods. Both have bolted flanges. A jacketed version keeps a product such as chocolate or syrup warm as it passes.

To clean, release the lid clamps, lift the rod cluster out and wipe the rods. Used for syrups, sauces, oils, coolants, hydraulic oil, liquid chemicals and slurries.
