## Tagline
A push sweeper that clears iron from floors and yards

## Bullets
- Permanent magnet bar between two rubber wheels
- 12 inch and 24 inch widths; wider builds to order
- Release lever drops the collected metal into a bin
- Handle 4 to 5 feet; steel or aluminium frame
- No power needed

## Overview
### Nails and swarf off the floor in one pass
Push the sweeper over the floor. Nails, screws, pins, swarf and wire lift onto the magnet bar. When the bar is full, hold it over a bin and pull the release lever. The load drops off. Nothing is picked up by hand.

Used on workshop and factory floors, in yards and loading bays, in car parks and on construction sites. It protects tyres and feet, and it saves the time spent sweeping and sorting by hand. A fork lift mounted sweeper is available for large yards.
