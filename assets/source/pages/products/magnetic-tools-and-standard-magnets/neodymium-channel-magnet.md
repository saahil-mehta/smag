## Overview
### Strong hold on one face
The steel channel wraps three sides of the magnet. It directs the field to the open face, so the pull on that face is higher than the bare magnet would give, and it protects the magnet from knocks.

Channel magnets hold signs, panels and covers on steel surfaces, mount fixtures in fabrication and assembly, and keep tools within reach in a workshop. Keep the working temperature below 80 degrees Celsius.
