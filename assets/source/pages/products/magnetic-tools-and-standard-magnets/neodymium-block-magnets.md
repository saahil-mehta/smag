## Overview
### The most pull in the least space
A neodymium block gives a strong, even field across a flat face. Blocks are used for clamping and fixtures, for holding sensors and covers, inside magnetic assemblies and separators, and wherever a small magnet must do a large job.

The plating protects the magnet from rust. Keep the working temperature below 80 degrees Celsius, as neodymium loses strength when hot. Standard sizes are stocked and other sizes are cut to order.
