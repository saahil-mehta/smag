## Overview
### Small discs, strong hold
A neodymium disc is magnetised through its thickness, so the pull is concentrated on the two flat faces. Discs are used in sensors, fixtures and jigs, in magnetic assemblies and in acoustic equipment.

The plating protects the magnet from rust. Keep the working temperature below 80 degrees Celsius. Standard sizes are stocked and other sizes are made to order.
