## Intro
### The sectors we build for
The same magnet does a different job in every plant. In a food line it keeps iron out of the product. In a steel yard it lifts plate. In a machine shop it holds the work and cleans the coolant.

We have supplied plants in these sectors since 1978. Pick yours to see where our equipment fits, or [contact us](/contact-us/) and describe the line.
