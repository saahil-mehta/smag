## Intro
### Fine iron out of powders, pastes and liquids
Chemical plants handle powders, granules, pastes and liquids that pick up iron from raw materials, from mills and mixers and from maintenance work. Iron in the product spoils colour and purity and can damage downstream equipment.

Our separators are made in 304 stainless steel with rods up to 13,500 gauss. Grids and housed grids handle dry product. Liquid traps handle solutions and slurries. Sizes are made to your line.

## Where
### Where the separators go
- Grids in intake hoppers and at sack tipping points
- Housed grids before and after sieving and mixing
- Drawer housings in pneumatic and gravity transfer lines
- Liquid traps in pipelines carrying solutions and slurries
- A high strength unit before bulk discharge or packing
