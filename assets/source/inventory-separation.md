- All surface parts food-quality 304 grade stainless steel, welded, fully sealed, highly polished
- Rod magnetic strengths: Standard 7,000-8,000 gauss; High 8,000-9,000; Super High 9,500-11,000; Extreme High 12,000-13,500

- Supplied with tested certificate and a guarantee on magnetic performance

## Housed easy-clean magnet separator

- Suggested slug: `housed-easy-clean-magnet-separator`
- A stainless steel gravity-fed separator with an enclosed magnet chamber mounted between an inlet hood and an outlet cone. The magnetic rods sit inside the sealed housing and pull ferrous contamination out of dry product as it falls through. The rod assembly withdraws for cleaning.
- Specs: 304 stainless steel housing, polished; NdFeB rod magnets; built to the customer's hopper or line dimensions.
- Applications: powders and granules in food, plastics, chemicals; fitted above processing machines and at intake or outlet points.
- Images: `housed-easy-clean-separator-01.png` (best hero shot: full unit, front on, white background).

## Easy-clean magnetic grid / grate separator

- Suggested slug: `easy-clean-magnetic-grid-separator`
- A two-stage grid in which the magnetic rods sit inside outer stainless tubes. Releasing the toggle clamps lifts the magnet core clear, so captured iron drops off the tubes without hand scraping. Mounting flange drilled to suit the hopper.
- Specs: 304 stainless steel; NdFeB cores; toggle-clamp quick release; flanged square frame.
- Applications: hoppers and chutes where frequent cleaning is needed; food and pharma lines.
- Images: `easy-clean-grid-separator-01.png` (best hero: flanged unit with red toggle clamps, magnet frame partly withdrawn), `easy-clean-grid-separator-02.png` (7-rod easy-clean grill, three-quarter view).

## Housed bullet / drawer magnetic separator (underflow, housed)

- Suggested slug: `housed-drawer-magnetic-separator`
- A flanged in-line housing with two sliding drawers of magnetic rods arranged in offset rows. Product falls through the staggered rods; each drawer pulls out through a clamped access door for cleaning. Made in painted mild steel or full stainless steel.
- Specs: double-drawer design; round inlet and outlet flanges; toggle-clamped door; NdFeB rods.
- Applications: enclosed transfer lines and pneumatic or gravity underflow ducts in milling, plastics and chemicals.
- Images: `double-drawer-housing-blue-01.png` (best hero: blue housed unit, door open, both drawers part drawn), `double-drawer-housing-ss-01.png` (all-stainless version with round flange, drawers extended).

## Magnetic grids for sieves / magnetic separation grids (round and square grills)

- Suggested slug: `magnetic-grills` (or split `round-magnetic-grills` / `square-magnetic-grills`)
- Frames of magnetic rods welded into a round or square stainless surround, sized to drop into a hopper, sieve or bin mouth. The rods trap nails, wire, swarf and other tramp iron from dry free-flowing product. Standard sizes ex stock, specials to drawing.
- Specs: 304 stainless steel throughout; NdFeB up to 11,000 gauss on the rod surface (ferrite versions available); one-stage and two-stage lattices; sizes seen: 6"x6", 8"x8", 12"x12" square; 10", 12", 15", 24" and 350 mm round; IndiaMART data adds magnet grades N35/N42/N45/N52, frame SS 304, diameters 6-26 inch.
- Applications (from SMAG's own leaflet): plastics, bakelite, flour and food grains, cement, stone, sand, fertilisers, chemicals, grindings, pigments, abrasives, carbon, coffee, glass, sugar, tea, spices.
- Recorded prices, do not publish: hopper magnetic grill Rs 2,500/piece; magnetic grill Rs 3,750/piece (IndiaMART).
- Images: `magnetic-grill-round-24in-01.png` (best hero for round: 24" two-stage grid, straight on), `magnetic-grill-round-350mm-01.png`, `magnetic-grill-round-10in-01.png`, `magnetic-grill-round-12in-01.png`, `magnetic-grill-round-15in-01.png`, `magnetic-grill-square-6in-01.png`, `magnetic-grill-square-8in-01.png`, `magnetic-grill-square-12in-01.png` (best hero for square), `magnetic-grill-rectangular-01.png` (tall rectangular multi-rod grill), `magnetic-grill-open-pair-01.png` (pair of open box grills with carry handles), `hopper-magnetic-grill-set-01.png` (three round grills grouped, IndiaMART listing photo), `hopper-magnetic-grill-set-02.png` (four sizes on blue cloth, real workshop photo), `magnetic-grill-round-workshop-01.png` (large round grill being handled; hands visible, secondary use only).

## Deep-field magnetic plate separator

- Suggested slug: `magnetic-plate-separator`
- Plate magnets in cast aluminium (LM-6) or encapsulated in SS 304/316, made in a range of sizes and magnetic depths for mounting over chutes and conveyors.
- Specs (from brochure "Magnetic Plate" panel): anisotropic ceramic magnets; LM-6 or SS 304/316 encapsulation; sizes to suit the required field strength and depth of penetration.
- Applications: textiles, tea, coffee, chemical and other process industries.

## Magnetic strip separator / hand magnetic separator

- Suggested slug: `hand-magnetic-separator`
- A hand-held plate magnet in an SS 304 cover with a release action, so tramp iron collected on the face detaches easily. Light and simple to use for spot checks and small batches. Made in rectangular and round shapes and in two sizes.
- Specs: strontium ferrite magnet, SS 304 easy-release cover, rectangular or round.
- Applications: foundries, plastics, scrap, chemicals; batch checking of raw material.
- Images: `hand-magnet-easy-clean-01.png` (hero: two sizes side by side on white).

## Magnetic sampling probe / easy-clean magnetic rod

- Suggested slug: `magnetic-sampling-rod`
- A T-handled magnetic rod with a sliding outer sleeve. Dipped into a bin or sack, it picks up ferrous fines; drawing the inner magnet out of the sleeve drops the captured iron. Also usable as a simple test probe for contamination checks.
- Specs: 304 stainless steel; NdFeB core; sealed and watertight.
- Images: `magnetic-sampling-rod-01.png` (hero: full rod with T-handle and slide plate).

## Neodymium magnetic rods / tubes (separator rods)

- Suggested slug: `neodymium-magnetic-rods`
- Sealed 304 stainless tubes containing rare earth NdFeB magnets and pole concentrators. Sold singly or assembled with side plates into grids, or fitted into an existing mechanical filter to form a combination filter. Fully welded and machined, watertight for liquid lines.
- Specs: 25.4 mm (1") standard diameter (a 25 mm x 18" rod is pictured); strengths from 7,000 up to 13,500 gauss by grade; round, square and triangle sections; N35/N45 NdFeB; ferrite (3,000 gauss) and Ferrearth (5,000 gauss) versions; do not expose above 100 deg C; every size customisable.
- Applications: sugar, grain, tea, plastic granulate, powdered chemicals, liquids; building blocks for OEM separator designs.
- Images: `neodymium-magnetic-rod-01.png` (hero: single plain rod, vertical on white).

## Magnetic filters / high-intensity magnetic liquid traps (Filtramag-type)

- Suggested slug: `magnetic-liquid-traps` (or `magnetic-filters`)
- Flanged stainless housings with a removable cluster of magnetic rods that sit in the liquid flow. Fine ferrous particles held on the rods are cleaned off when the magnet assembly is lifted out. Two IndiaMART-listed builds: an in-line trap and a larger basket-style housing.
- Specs: neodymium bars; listed sizes: 75 mm prong diameter x 250 mm with 7 bars, and 150 mm prong diameter x 250 mm with 9 bars; flanged connections.
- Applications (from listing): lubricants, coolants, hydraulic oils, slurries, liquid chemicals, liquid food products.
- Recorded price, do not publish: Rs 35,000/piece (IndiaMART).
- Images: `magnetic-liquid-trap-inline-01.png` (best hero: dismantled in-line trap showing flanged tee body and two rod clusters), `magnetic-liquid-trap-housed-01.png` (basket-style housed filter; slightly soft at 500 px).

## Overband magnetic separator

- Suggested slug: `overband-magnetic-separator`
- A permanent magnet block with a driven belt running around it, suspended above a conveyor. The belt continuously throws off captured tramp iron such as nuts, bolts, rivets and tool pieces, keeping the magnet face clear without stopping the line.
- Specs (IndiaMART): magnet grade, size, type and finish built to requirement; export packing; made in India.
- Applications: grain, feedstuffs, raw sugar, dry chemicals, salt, ceramics, cement clinker, coal conveying in power plants.
- Recorded price, do not publish: Rs 35,000 onwards (IndiaMART).
- Images: `overband-magnetic-separator-01.png` (hero by default; looks like a catalogue render rather than a factory photo, 500 px).
