About SMAG

A Mumbai magnet workshop, on production lines since 1978

We design, machine and test every unit at our own works in Dahisar, Mumbai. Nothing leaves until it has been matched to the job it is going to do. We have worked this way since 1978, for plants across India and for export.

Every unit is sized from the application itself. Your material, your throughput and your load decide the pull, the reach and the housing we build.

Built in-house, matched to the job

Magnet circuits, housings and machined faces are all made here, which puts build quality and delivery dates in our own hands. Inspection and dispatch run under an ISO 9001:2015 system.

Years after a unit ships we still carry spares for it, re-magnetise assemblies that have weakened, and service what we sold.

How we specify a magnet

The same three steps for one stock magnet and for a separator built around your line.

01. Read the application

What is the material, how fast is it moving, how much room is there, and what are you trying to catch or lift.

02. Match the circuit

Pull, reach, pole layout and housing get sized to those answers and drawn up for your line.

03. Build, test and dispatch

Made, tested and packed here. Servicing and spares later come from the same workshop.
