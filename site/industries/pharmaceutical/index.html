Pharmaceuticals

Home /  Industries / Pharmaceuticals

Clean product, clean records

Pharmaceutical lines handle powders, granules, tablets and liquids where any metal is a serious defect. Iron arrives with raw materials and comes off mills, blenders and tablet presses as they wear.

Our separators are made in polished 304 stainless steel with rods up to 13,500 gauss. They hold fine particles and work hardened stainless steel. Every unit is supplied with a tested certificate of magnetic strength, and a gauss meter lets you re-check it for your validation records.
