# Surface brief: SMAG site (all templates)

Scope: the whole site, 66 pages sharing one theme stylesheet. Home, families and industries are Persuade; product pages are Persuade with Operate detail (specs); guides are Read. Visitors are buyers and export customers; success is a call or WhatsApp message.

Pinned (PRODUCT.md): S-MAG red and logo, Noto Sans, every word of copy, existing photography. Never cold, never flashy, contact always in reach, specs never buried.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is SMAG's own range book. Every product is a plate on white with its readings beside it. It refuses the Eclipse template: overlaid hero headline, red-barred card grid, stats row, red banner.

OWN-WORLD: White pages and cool steel #F2F4F5 bands, ink #16181A, secondary #5B6268, 1px #D9DDE0 rules, square corners, no shadows. Red #E20026 is reserved for the action. Noto Sans with hierarchy by scale contrast on an 8px baseline; tabular numerals, one unit style. Components: framed product plates with a ruled caption, ruled reading tables, a thumb-index of the six families, an ink back cover.

STORY: The buyer sees a real Mumbai works, finds the family, reads the specification, and calls or messages without hunting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The hero film as a full-bleed plate at its own 1224 x 720 proportion, up to the full screen (the user rejected a shorter, cropped hero on 30 Sep 2026; the family plates now begin just below it). The existing headline is set large in white on its lower left inside a quiet title block, with the primary button directly below it (beside it, the button detached from a three-line headline). A thin scroll cue breathes at the foot. The header carries the lockup, the nav, and the call and WhatsApp actions.

FORM: The Works Catalogue, candidate 3 of 7 (seed b9a360ff), raised by type specimen (scale-contrast hierarchy), oscilloscope (specs as readings), pulp rack (face-out plates plus a spine-out range index), cityscape (red reserved for the action), teen quiz (every spread ends at call and WhatsApp). Signature interaction: the thumb-index, a family index that marks where you are on family and product pages. Motion (the user asked for subtleties on 30 Sep 2026): the hero title then its button settle on load; content below the fold rises into place once as it enters, siblings in sequence; images fade in as they load; plates and photos ease forward a few percent on hover with the frame darkening; nav and index links draw an underline; arrows nudge; client logos turn from grey to colour; the sticky header slides down. Exponential ease-out throughout, no parallax, no scroll-linked effects, all off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
